About

A statistician across disciplines and borders.

I am a Houston-based statistician with a PhD from the University of São Paulo. My work connects statistical theory to applied questions in medicine, public health, and healthcare AI. I have taught statistics in Brazil and worked with research teams in the United States and Peru.

I welcome collaborations and methods consulting where careful analysis can improve how evidence is produced and used.
